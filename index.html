About

Curiosity, turned into a job

I've always wondered how the things we all use every day actually work, starting with apps. That question is what led me to technology.

I graduated in Computer Science and Telecommunications from ITI Cannizzaro with 100/100. During my studies I led the team that built a waste-sorting robot, presented in Rome at the “Scuola Futura” event at the invitation of the Italian Ministry of Education. In the same period I had my first work experience at Bcame, writing automated tests for the e-commerce site of a well-known Italian fashion brand.

After graduating I joined Tri-Tech Solutions, working on cloud data engineering projects for multinational clients. Today, with IF Consulting, I work on an application support project for STMicroelectronics.

Outside work I'm passionate about Italian dubbing, cinema and 3D animation, 17th and 18th-century history, and management games.
